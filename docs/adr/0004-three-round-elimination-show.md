# Three-round elimination show with a practice safety net

A Show always follows Course → survival → final, with a shrinking lobby (16 → 8 → 4 → 1). Elimination is real: falling or touching slime removes a Tumbler, and the player spectates the remainder. Respawn checkpoints would protect the player but would remove the stakes that make a finish line mean anything, so the safety net is a separate Practice mode with respawns and no elimination. The fixed arc gives every session a beginning, a reversal, and a crowning moment, and guarantees the show ends even if the pack collapses.

**Consequences:** an early fall means a short spectate session; every Round needs explicit tie-breaks and a zero-survivor fallback; Practice and Show share level code but not the elimination policy.
